# Security

Do not open a public GitHub issue for a suspected vulnerability, exposed secret, or customer-identifying information.

Contact N2con through an established private support or business channel and include:

- the affected file and commit or release;
- the behavior you observed;
- steps to reproduce the issue; and
- any relevant signature or file-hash results.

N2con may remove an affected artifact while it is investigated. Consumers should pin approved versions or hashes where their deployment platform allows it.
